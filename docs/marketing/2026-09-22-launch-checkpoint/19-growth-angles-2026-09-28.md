# Growth angles — draft, angle and audience only, 2026-09-28

Per `idea-pl-growth-gift-a-line-and-airtime-home-2026-09-28` and `dec-owner-pl-reprice-overage-on-cost-and-popularity-2026-09-28`
(Open — a proposal, not yet ratified). **No claims, no prices, nothing published.** Where the product flow
or pricing doesn't exist yet, it's said plainly, not implied.

## 1. "Gift a Dominican number"

**Angle:** flip the frame from "get your own number" to giving one — a diaspora customer sets up and pays
for a Personal Line for family still in Dominica, so the payer is abroad and the user is at home. Natural
occasions: reassurance ("always reachable"), holidays, birthdays, homecoming gifts.

**Audience:** diaspora Dominicans (US/Canada) with family in Dominica.

**Not built yet — flagged, not glossed over:** the product has no payer≠user relationship and no
recipient invite/activation flow. This is angle and audience only; no campaign can promise this until it's
real.

## 2. "Call home + send airtime home" — migrant communities living in Dominica

**Angle:** pair an eventual Dominica-based call-home rate with the *existing* airtime-send capability
(bff-v2's `/api/lite/airtime` path — detect, options, quote, pay-link, send), which Reloadly periodically
runs operator bonus promotions on. Working line, not for use yet: "call home cheap, and send airtime too."

**Primary audience:** the Haitian community living in Dominica. **Secondary:** Dominican Republic,
Venezuelan, Cuban and Guyanese communities in Dominica.

**Open, per the idea record itself — needs checking before any claim is possible:** which Reloadly
operators/countries the live airtime path actually supports today (Haiti Digicel/Natcom, DR Claro/Altice,
Venezuela, Cuba Cubacel), current margin/fees, how bonuses actually display, whether airtime-send works in
production today, and which lane owns it. Call-home pricing itself is pending the owner
(`dec-owner-pl-reprice-overage-on-cost-and-popularity-2026-09-28`, still Open). **None of this is verified —
flagging the exact open questions rather than assuming answers.**

## 3. Three-segment positioning — one sentence each, per the Open repricing decision

- **Locals** (Dominica-resident, calling locally + US/Canada, including US toll-free): great rates for
  calling around Dominica and to the US/Canada — priced for what people actually call, not matched to a
  competitor's benchmark.
- **Diaspora** (abroad, calling home): stay reachable on your own Dominican number wherever you live, with
  competitive international calling and no roaming.
- **Migrants living in Dominica** (calling home to their own country): call home to the Dominican Republic,
  Haiti, Cuba, Venezuela and similar — affordably, from the country you live in now.

No rate figures in any of the three — the repricing decision itself is still Open.

## Languages and channels for these communities

**Languages:** Haitian Creole for the Haitian-community angle specifically; Spanish for the Dominican
Republic/Venezuelan/Cuban-community angle. The core diaspora (US/Canada) campaign stays in English.

**Channels:** WhatsApp groups (community and diaspora groups are the one channel this whole campaign
already relies on), churches (a genuine community hub for migrant populations, especially the Haitian
community in Dominica), and remittance/money-transfer shops (a natural physical adjacency to "send airtime
home" — people already visit these in person to send money).
